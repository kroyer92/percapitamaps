How it works

1Join here. You get the next citizen number, for good.
2Every day the census counts everyone and averages the real World Bank figures of your home countries. The Short shows where the nation would rank among real countries, and which real country it has to pass next.
3Once we have enough citizens, vote here on the nation's flag, then its capital, motto and more, one at a time. Each result is announced in the Short.
Privacy

We keep only the name you type and the country you pick, with your citizen number and the time you joined. They are shown on this page and may be shown in the daily video on YouTube and Instagram. No email, no login, no tracking cookies. Your browser keeps a private code so it can show your passport and let you leave.

To stop one connection making many passports or votes, a salted one-way hash of your internet address is kept for 2 days (sign-ups), or until the question is decided (votes), then deleted. Nobody can turn it back into the address.

Leave any time with the "Leave the nation" button: your passport is deleted at once (videos already published can't be changed). Or ask in a comment on any Per Capita Maps video and we'll delete it.
